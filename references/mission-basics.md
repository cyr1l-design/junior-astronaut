# Mission basics (not from the challenge resources)

The brief mentions these, but its Resources tab has no dataset for them. These
are standard NASA figures; **cite a NASA page before showing them in the game**.

| Thing | Value | For a kid |
|---|---|---|
| **Sol** (a Mars day) | 24 h 39 min 35 s | A Mars day is about 40 minutes longer than ours. |
| Moon day | ~29.5 Earth days, sunrise to sunrise | About two weeks of sunlight, then two weeks of night: hard on solar power. |
| Comms delay, Moon ↔ Earth | ~1.3 seconds each way | Almost a normal phone call. |
| Comms delay, Mars ↔ Earth | ~3 to ~22 minutes each way, depending on where the planets are | You can't ask Earth for help in an emergency; you decide yourself. |
| Mars gravity | ~38% of Earth's | You'd weigh about a third as much. |
| Moon gravity | ~17% (one-sixth) of Earth's | You'd weigh a sixth as much. |

Microgravity (on the ISS, in transit) and low gravity (on the Moon or Mars) both
weaken muscle and bone, which links to [health-and-food.md](health-and-food.md).
