## Notes on `base-location-AI-reference.webp` for the 3D build

Keep: black sky, sharp shadows, red-brown salt cracks against pale ice, chaos
blocks on the right, double ridge with the glowing cave on the left, Io near
Jupiter, distant ice blades, faint purple aurora, cables crossing a red crack,
cable spool, cryobot over its hole.

Fix when building:
- **Jupiter's lit side must face the Sun.** Here the Sun is low on the right
  but Jupiter is lit from the front-left. In 3D, lighting Jupiter with the same
  Sun fixes this automatically.
- **The reactor has solar wings.** Reactors carry radiator fins; the solar
  panels belong in the separate array.
- **The melt hole shows a glowing pool of liquid.** Open water can't sit on
  Europa's surface in a vacuum; show a glow of heat in an ice shaft instead.
- The Sun is drawn a little large; from Europa it's a tiny brilliant point.
